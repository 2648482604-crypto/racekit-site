Race day

Working back from race day

We start from the day the medals must be in your hands and plan backwards. This is the order every medal order follows.

  1. 1

    You send the brief

    Your artwork or a written brief, the quantity, the event date and the delivery address.

  2. 2

    Free artwork mock-up

    We draw the medal and ribbon for you to check and correct. No charge for the mock-up.

  3. 3

    Artwork locked, order confirmed

    Aim to lock the design at least 8 weeks before your delivery date.

  4. 4

    Pre-production sample

    A real sample made before bulk production. Bulk starts only after you sign it off.

  5. 5

    Bulk production & checks

    Medals made to the signed sample, checked, and bagged one by one.

  6. 6

    Shipped by sea

    Sea freight, then delivery to your door.

  7. 7

    Delivered before race day

    To one address in Kuala Lumpur or the Klang Valley.

The plain facts

Where it's made
At a specialist medal factory in China, then shipped to Malaysia and delivered to one address in Kuala Lumpur or the Klang Valley.
What you approve
A free artwork mock-up first, then a physical pre-production sample. Bulk follows the sample you signed.
How long
About 6–8 weeks door to door by sea once artwork is final and the order is confirmed. Fixed only when we confirm it in writing.
How payment works
50% deposit when you confirm the order; the balance on the terms in your formal quotation.
